Pinning rationale: SP 800-61 Rev. 3 supersedes Rev. 2 and aligns incident response with CSF 2.0. Other listed publications are stable NIST sources for RMF, control selection, and risk assessment.

## Scope Notes

Use this skill for governance, risk, compliance, control selection, incident response planning, and evidence-oriented assessment. Use `software-supply-chain-security` when the primary concern is secure development, provenance, SBOM, CI/CD integrity, dependency hygiene, or artifact trust.

## ID Conventions

- NIST CSF 2.0 functions use `GV`, `ID`, `PR`, `DE`, `RS`, and `RC`.
- NIST CSF categories and subcategories use dot notation such as `GV.RM` and `GV.RM-01`.
- SP 800-53 controls use family and number notation such as `AC-2`, with enhancements such as `AC-2(1)`.
- NIST SP publications should include revision numbers when cited formally.

## Validation Guidance

Validate exact control IDs and publication revisions when producing formal audit or compliance materials. Treat NIST guidance as assessment support unless a governing authority, contract, or regulation makes it mandatory for the scope.
