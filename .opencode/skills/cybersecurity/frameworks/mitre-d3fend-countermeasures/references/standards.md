## Scope Notes

Use D3FEND to map offensive behaviors and affected artifacts to defensive countermeasures. Focus on how a control works, what artifact it acts on, and what evidence proves it is operating.

## ID Conventions

- Defensive techniques commonly use `D3-*` identifiers.
- Offensive references may point back to ATT&CK, ATLAS, Mobile, ICS, or related frameworks.

## Validation Guidance

Do not invent D3FEND IDs. Validate exact technique names and identifiers through the official site or ontology artifacts when precision matters.
