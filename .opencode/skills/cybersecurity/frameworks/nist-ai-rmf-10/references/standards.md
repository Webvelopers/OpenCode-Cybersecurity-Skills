# NIST AI RMF References

Target version: 1.0.

Last verified: 2026-09-08.

Pinning rationale: NIST AI RMF 1.0 is the stable baseline for AI risk management. Use the NIST Generative AI Profile when the system involves GenAI-specific risks.

## Official Sources

- NIST AI RMF page: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- AI RMF knowledge base: https://airc.nist.gov/AI_RMF_Knowledge_Base/AI_RMF
- AI RMF 1.0 publication: https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.100-1
- Generative AI profile: https://doi.org/10.6028/NIST.AI.600-1

## Scope Notes

Use AI RMF for AI lifecycle risk management, governance, measurement, trustworthiness, and residual risk decisions. Use the Generative AI profile when the system involves GenAI-specific risks.

## ID Conventions

- Core functions are Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage.
- Categories and subcategories should be cited exactly only after validation against official NIST materials.

## Validation Guidance

Separate AI governance risk, model risk, data risk, operational risk, cybersecurity risk, and adversarial AI risk. Use ATLAS when adversary behavior mapping is required and OWASP GenAI when application-level LLM risks are in scope.
