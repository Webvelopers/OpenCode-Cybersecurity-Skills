## Scope Notes

Use CIS for secure configuration baselines, hardening priorities, implementation groups, benchmark checks, exceptions, and evidence collection. CIS guidance should be adapted to the target environment's criticality, regulatory needs, and operational constraints.

## ID Conventions

- CIS Controls are commonly referenced by control and safeguard numbers.
- Implementation Groups are `IG1`, `IG2`, and `IG3`.
- CIS Benchmarks are product and version specific; include benchmark title, profile or level, section, and recommendation ID when citing.
- Benchmark recommendations may include Level 1 or Level 2 profiles depending on platform and risk.

## Validation Guidance

Validate benchmark recommendations against the exact platform, product version, and operational constraints before applying hardening changes. Record exceptions with owner, business rationale, compensating controls, and review date.
